# Guide to Exercise 3

In [](Exercise_3), the {ref}`Problem 1<Prob_3-1>` is a simple derivation of the volume of phase space. Note that, this volume is just a unit to make sure the phase space volume is defined accordingly such that it is reasonable to put it in the Log function. Others are just detail.
The {ref}`Problem 2<Prob_3-2>` is a simple question about the trajectory of a system in the phase space.
The {ref}`Problem 3<Prob_3-3>` is about the role of phase space volume at equilibrium.
The {ref}`Problem 4<Prob_3-4>` is a different example of microcanonical ensemble beyond the ideal gas model.
The {ref}`Problem 5<Prob_3-5>` is an intuition why non-interacting systems are special: they automatically lead to factorizable partition function. Therefore, increasing number of degree of freedom doesn't make the system more complex. Here, we want to contrast the case for generic interacting system and ask how the partition function differs from that perspective.
The {ref}`Problem 6<Prob_3-6>` is a problem about partition function of H-atom. It is the point where knowing what we are doing is very useful. Try to distinguish the physical reality and the mathematical description and identify the blind spot where staring at the equation alone is not useful.
